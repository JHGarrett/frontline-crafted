# The Arden — Two-Species Hardwood and Plywood Costs

Internal reference only. Not imported or displayed by the website.

Prepared October 6, 2026. Estimates depend on actual lumber and panels selected. Excludes tax, finish, glue, abrasives, drawer slides, pulls, fasteners, delivery, labor, and overhead.

## Design requirement: two hardwood species

The Arden requires two different hardwood species. The 5/4 and 6/4 must match each other.

For the provisional combination tables, all 32 BF of 4/4 is assigned to the other species. John has not yet explicitly confirmed whether some of the 4/4 should also match the 5/4 and 6/4. Confirm that allocation before quoting; if the 4/4 is split, recalculate using each species' actual BF.

## Build requirements supplied by John

- 4/4 hardwood: 32 BF for cabinet pieces, shelf, and back panel.
- 5/4 hardwood: 4 BF for cabinet pieces; same species as the 6/4.
- 6/4 hardwood: 4 BF for base pieces.
- Total hardwood: 40 BF.
- Plywood: one 1/2-inch sheet and one 1/4-inch sheet for drawer boxes, back panel, and jigs. Birch or another economical plywood is acceptable. No MDF core.
- Sheet calculations assume 4 × 8 feet and sufficient cut yield.

The back panel appears in both the hardwood and plywood descriptions. The tables retain all supplied quantities; confirm whether these describe different panels or alternatives so the final estimate does not double-count material.
