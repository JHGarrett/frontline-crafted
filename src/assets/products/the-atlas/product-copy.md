# The Atlas

Sculptural Coffee Table

Built to fit your space.

Starting at $700. Made to order in Parker County, Texas.

## Introduction

A quiet statement for your living room. The Atlas pairs a broad, beveled tabletop with tapered, splayed legs and a solid-wood center stretcher. Its low profile and strong angles give it an architectural presence, with carefully shaped details that reward a closer look.

## Crafted with intention

The Atlas balances a simple top with an expressive base. Tapered legs, shaped edges, and an angled stance bring character to the piece while keeping its proportions clean and restrained.

Choose the standard 12-inch height for a distinctly low profile, or a 15-inch version for a taller coffee table. Each piece is handcrafted one at a time to suit your room.

## Dimensions and materials

- Standard size: approximately 54 inches long × 24 inches wide × 12 inches tall.
- Alternative height: 15 inches.
- Solid wood construction with a sculptural splayed-leg base and beveled tabletop detail.
- Custom lengths, widths, heights, and edge profiles available.
- Multiple wood species, custom stains, and natural finishes available.
- Premium hardwood upgrades include walnut, white oak, red oak, maple, and beech.

## Made for your space

From a compact living room to a generous sectional, The Atlas can be built around your layout. Share your preferred dimensions, wood, and finish, and we’ll work through the options together. Matching side tables, media consoles, and shelving can also be commissioned.
