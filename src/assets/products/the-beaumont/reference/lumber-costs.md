- Cabinet carcass, drawer fronts, and door: 40 BF of 4/4 hardwood for the hardwood version. 3/4-inch plywood may be used for some components; John notes that only a small amount of this plywood is needed.

- Drawer boxes and back panel: two sheets of nominal 1/2-inch BB plywood, interpreted here as Baltic birch, consistent with the preceding Langston estimate. Confirm this interpretation before ordering.

## Customer decision: choose one base

**Ask the customer which base design they want, then price that option accordingly.** The 8/4 non-template base and the 6/4 template base are alternatives; do not add both to the quote.

- Hardwood build with non-template base: 47 BF total.
- Hardwood build with template base: 46 BF total.

A two-hardwood-species requirement has not been specified for this product. The tables assume the selected species for cabinet and base.

## Small 3/4-inch plywood option — customer quote adjustment

Only a small amount of 3/4-inch plywood is required if that option is used. No cut sizes, square footage, species, or allocation of replaced 4/4 hardwood has been supplied. **Do not assume a full sheet purchase or replace all 40 BF of hardwood in the estimate.**

Before the customer quote:

1. Identify the components made from plywood and their required cut sizes.
2. Determine the 4/4 BF those components replace; retain hardwood needed for fronts, door, edging, and other components.
3. Select non-MDF-core plywood of the appropriate face species/finish and allocate its material cost, including any shop offcuts used.
4. Subtract only the replaced hardwood cost and add the plywood allowance. If a full sheet must be purchased, use the actual purchase cost instead.

No small 3/4-inch plywood cost is included in the tables; add it and adjust the hardwood quantities before issuing the customer quote. Species matching for this optional plywood has not been specified; confirm the intended appearance.
