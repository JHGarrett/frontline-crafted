# The Belmont — Hardwood and Panel Costs

Internal reference only. Not imported or displayed by the website.

Recorded October 5, 2026, from supplier listings retrieved during planning. Calculated costs depend on the actual boards and panels selected. All prices are before tax and exclude finish, glue, abrasives, drawer slides, pulls, fasteners, delivery, labor, and overhead.

## Design requirement: two hardwood species

This build requires two different hardwood species, such as cherry and walnut; those are examples, not a fixed pairing. Confirm the chosen pairing and which components use each species before quoting.

The current 26 BF of 4/4 and 33 BF of 5/4 have not been allocated between the two species. Existing single-species hardwood totals and combined plywood tables are comparison baselines, not complete estimates for the required two-species build. A final estimate needs the BF of each thickness for each species. Plywood choices are separate from this hardwood requirement.

## Build requirements supplied by John

- 4/4 hardwood: 26 BF for drawer faces, drawer boxes, and edge banding.
- 5/4 hardwood: 33 BF for exterior case and base.
- 1/4-inch panel: one sheet for drawer bottoms and back panel.
- 3/4-inch panel: two sheets for interior cabinet.
- Sheet calculations assume 4 × 8 feet and sufficient cut yield; confirm the cutting layout.
- Existing hardwood comparison totals use one species for all 59 BF. The required two-species build needs a separate allocation before its final cost can be calculated.
