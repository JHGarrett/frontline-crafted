- [Walnut rough lumber](https://www.plywoodcompany.com/pl/lumber-moulding-hardwood-rough-random-widths-lengths-walnut/765?sort=Name): 5/4 $15.26/BF, 6/4 $15.79/BF, 8/4 $15.61/BF.

Maple uses the Maple White FAS 5/4 and 6/4 listings (54MAPLE, 64MAPLE), and Maple 8/4 (84MAPLE), rather than the separate hard maple listing. Confirm matching species before purchasing. White oak uses regular rough lumber, not rift or quarter-sawn.

Walnut 8/4 uses $15.61/BF, verified earlier on October 5 for the Carrington, Atlas, and Rowan checks. The latest Bennett listing did not display that item's price; reconfirm before quoting.

Rough lumber comes in random widths and lengths. Confirm usable dimensions and required finished thickness; purchased footage may exceed this estimate. Recheck prices and stock before quoting a customer.
