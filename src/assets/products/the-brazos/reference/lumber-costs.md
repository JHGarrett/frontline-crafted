# The Brazos — Lumber Costs for Listed Size

Internal reference only. Not imported or displayed by the website.

Prepared October 6, 2026, using supplier rates verified during October 5–6 planning. Calculated costs depend on actual lumber selected. Reconfirm prices before quoting.

## Build requirements supplied by John

- Tabletop: 40 BF of 5/4 hardwood.
- Base pieces: 11 BF of 6/4 hardwood.
- Total: 51 BF.

These quantities are based on the product's listed standard dimensions: approximately 96 inches long × 43 inches wide × 29½ inches tall. The Brazos is customizable to the customer's sizing needs. **Confirm the requested dimensions and recalculate the cutting layout and board footage before quoting a custom size.** Do not assume that the standard 51 BF applies to every size.

A two-species requirement has not been specified. The tables assume the chosen species for both tabletop and base.

## Base lumber purchasing note

**Try to buy the 6/4 base lumber in either 4½-inch or 8-inch widths to reduce waste.** Confirm the cut layout and usable width after milling when selecting boards. These are preferred purchasing widths, not a guarantee of supplier stock or a replacement for actual board selection. Request the widths when obtaining a quote.
