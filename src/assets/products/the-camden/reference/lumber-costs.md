## Standard plan scope and sizing

The guide specifies 72-inch-long top and bottom panels, oversized to 17 inches wide, with cabinet panels finishing approximately 1⅛ inches thick (page 5). Final cuts and joinery refer to separate DIMENSIONS documentation, which was not supplied. Confirm that document and the customer's finished dimensions before building or quoting a custom size. Do not treat the rough purchase dimensions below as finished furniture dimensions.

A two-species hardwood requirement has not been specified. Comparison totals assume the same species for all hardwood; allocate separately if contrasting wood is requested.

## Suggested hardwood purchase — page 3

Widths are **combined widths across multiple boards**, not single-board width requirements. For example, a 21-inch total width can be three 7-inch boards. Each illustrated purchase blank is 96 inches long.

| **Total hardwood** |                                                                 |                                                      | **67⅓ BF** |

Board feet = rough thickness in inches × width in inches × length in inches ÷ 144. Use the inch dimensions rather than mixing the rounded metric labels into the calculation. The door frame comes from the 6/4 offcuts; do not add a second complete frame allowance without checking yield. The frame finishes at 1 inch thick (page 21).

The guide's purchase layout already includes excess around the parts. An optional additional 15% is shown for comparison, not automatically required. The slat blank's actual usable yield should be checked against the final door layout.

## Sheet goods — page 4

| Material                 | Required rough panel size |    Area | Requirement     |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------- | ------: | --------------- |
| Nominal 1/4-inch plywood | 72 × 18 inches            | 9 sq ft | Cabinet backing |
| Nominal 3/4-inch plywood | 32 × 18 inches            | 4 sq ft | Optional shelf  |

These are partial panels, not automatically full-sheet requirements. One 4×8 sheet of each thickness is sufficient for these individual blanks if full-sheet purchases are needed; use a cut layout to confirm. Shop offcuts can be used if adequate, but include their allocated material cost in customer quotes.

The guide pairs 1/4 inch with 5 mm and 3/4 inch with 19 mm. These are approximate labels, not exact equivalents. Confirm actual thickness and adjust rabbets, shelf details, and joinery. Use plywood without MDF core.

The optional shelf also needs solid hardwood edging on the front and sides (page 14), with dowels, biscuits, or Dominos for alignment. An exact rough edging allowance is not provided. Verify whether existing offcuts cover it; otherwise add edging material to the quote. The shelf can instead be solid wood, requiring a separate material allowance.

## Hardware and consumables

The guide calls for the following, without complete counts or prices:

- Cabinet joinery: 8 mm Dominos or 10 mm / 3/8-inch dowels (page 6).
- Base stretcher joinery: 10 mm Dominos or dowels (page 17).
- Sliding-door frame: 8 mm Dominos or dowels (page 22).
- Screws for the backing (page 12).
- Base-to-cabinet screws and washers, with movement clearance; low-profile cabinet screws suggested (pages 18 and 20).
- Shelf pins and sleeves if an adjustable shelf is chosen (page 13); determine count from final layout.
- Wood glue or assembly epoxy, finish, abrasives, and jig/test-door material.
- Optional reinforcing dowels or brass rod through base joints (page 19).

The door uses wooden tongues sliding in cabinet grooves; the guide does not specify purchased sliding-door track hardware. Do not add a track kit unless the build is changed to use one. Router templates and practice pieces may need additional scrap allocation.

## Plywood pricing approach

Saved economical imported birch rates: 5.2 mm 4×8 sheet $26.09; 18 mm 4×8 sheet $63.54 (verified October 5). These are thinner than the nominal panel requirements and are only alternatives subject to approval of thickness, core, and final layout.

- Full-sheet purchasing allowance for both metric alternatives: **$89.63**, with leftover material remaining for other jobs.
- Area-proportioned reference for the required pieces only: 9/32 × $26.09 = **$7.34** for backing; 4/32 × $63.54 = **$7.94** for optional shelf. Combined allocated panel reference: **$15.28**, computed before rounding. This is not a supplier cut-panel quote and excludes kerf, unusable remnants, and handling.
- Saved nominal 1/4-inch maple veneer-core 4×8 sheet: $59.20, an alternative backing choice. Its 9 sq ft area allocation would be $16.65. Confirm sheet purchase versus offcut allocation.

Do not add the optional shelf cost if it is omitted. For exact nominal thicknesses, other species, or supplier-cut panels, obtain the appropriate quotes. No complete material total is claimed until sheet choices, purchased versus allocated costs, shelf choice, edging yield, hardware, and consumables are settled.
