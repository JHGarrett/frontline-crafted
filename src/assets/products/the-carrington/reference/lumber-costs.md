# The Carrington — Basic Version Lumber Costs

Internal reference only. Not imported or displayed by the website.

Recorded October 5, 2026, from the supplier rates checked during planning. These are calculated lumber costs from the supplier’s listed rates. Your final invoice will depend on the actual boards selected.

## Board requirements

- Top: six 8/4 boards, each 8 inches wide × 108 inches long — 72 board feet.
- Basic base: three 6/4 boards, each 9 inches wide × 96 inches long — 27 board feet.
- Total: 99 board feet. The optional 15% extra is a purchasing allowance for defects and board selection.
