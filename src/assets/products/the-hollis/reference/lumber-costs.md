Internal reference only. Not imported or displayed by the website.

## Design requirement: two hardwood species

This build requires two different hardwood species, such as cherry and walnut; those are examples, not a fixed pairing.

The primary species must match across the two 4/4 boards and the one 8/4 board. The remaining 4/4 board must be a different, contrasting species. The combination tables already account for these allocations and exclude same-species pairings. Confirm the selected pairing before quoting.

## Boards supplied by John

- Matching primary species: two 4/4 boards, each 8 inches × 10 feet.
- Matching primary species: one 8/4 board, 8 inches × 10 feet.

Board feet = thickness in inches × width in inches × length in feet ÷ 12.
