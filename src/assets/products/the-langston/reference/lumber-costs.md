## Build requirements supplied by John

- 4/4 hardwood: 38 BF for cabinet, drawer faces, and shelving.
- 5/4 hardwood: 25 BF for tabletop and aprons.
- 8/4 hardwood: 8 BF for legs.
- Total hardwood for solid-cabinet build: 71 BF.
- Drawer boxes: one sheet of nominal 1/2-inch Baltic birch plywood.
- Alternative cabinet: one sheet of nominal 3/4-inch plywood with a face species matching the hardwood. No MDF core.

A two-hardwood-species requirement has not been specified. Matching plywood refers to the visible face species; its internal veneer core does not need to be that species.

### Cabinet allocation needed before totaling

The 38 BF of 4/4 includes cabinet, drawer faces, and shelving. The cabinet's share has not been supplied. **Do not subtract all 38 BF when choosing the plywood cabinet**, because the drawer faces and remaining hardwood shelving still need material.

Let C = BF of 4/4 replaced by the plywood cabinet, R4/R5/R8 = the selected species' lumber rates, P = matching cabinet sheet price, and B = chosen Baltic birch sheet price.

- Solid-cabinet total = 38 × R4 + 25 × R5 + 8 × R8 + B.
- Plywood-cabinet total = (38 − C) × R4 + 25 × R5 + 8 × R8 + P + B.
- Difference from solid cabinet = P − C × R4.
- With optional 15% extra hardwood: multiply the hardwood portion by 1.15, then add P + B.

Keep required solid edging and any hardwood shelving in the retained quantity. Reconfirm C if the plywood also replaces shelving. A final plywood-version total is pending this allocation and any missing supplier rates.
