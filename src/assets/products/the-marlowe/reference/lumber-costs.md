Internal reference only. Not imported or displayed by the website.

Prepared October 6, 2026. Calculated lumber costs depend on the actual boards selected. Quantities are assumed for one nightstand; multiply and check cut yield for matching pairs.

## Boards supplied by John

- 4/4: two boards, each 8 inches wide × 108 inches long.
- 5/4: one board, 8 inches wide × 48 inches long.
- Small amounts of 1/2-inch and 1/4-inch plywood, expected to come from shop scraps.

Board feet = thickness in inches × width in inches × length in inches ÷ 144.

## Plywood — excluded from this reference total

Small amounts of **1/2-inch and 1/4-inch plywood are required**. John expects to use available shop scraps, so no plywood cost is included in the lumber figures above.

**Add a plywood material allowance to the customer quote**, even when the pieces come from shop scraps. Determine the cut sizes, required quality, usable scrap availability, and allocated cost when quoting. This estimate does not treat plywood as unnecessary or automatically free. Use plywood without MDF core, consistent with the established preference.

## Allowances and exclusions

The optional 15% extra is a purchasing allowance for defects and board selection. Do not add it again if the listed boards already provide sufficient waste. Actual purchased board lengths and widths may increase the invoice. Confirm whether the 8-inch widths are rough or required usable widths after milling.

Totals use exact board footage before rounding; rounded component lines can differ by a penny. Figures exclude plywood, tax, finish, glue, abrasives, drawer slides, pulls, fasteners, delivery, labor, and overhead.
